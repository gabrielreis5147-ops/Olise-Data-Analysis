# Olist Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the **Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset**, sourced from Kaggle, to uncover meaningful business insights and identify opportunities for improvement.

Rather than simply exploring the data or presenting descriptive statistics, the main goal is to approach the dataset from a business perspective, transforming raw data into actionable insights that can support better decision-making.

## Objectives

- Identify business opportunities through data analysis.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and patterns.
- Evaluate sales performance and business trends.
- Investigate potential operational bottlenecks.
- Generate data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.

## Business Approach

The analysis goes beyond answering *"What happened?"* and focuses on questions such as:

- **Why did it happen?**
- **What opportunities can be identified?**
- **How can the business improve based on these findings?**

The ultimate goal is to translate data into valuable business insights and actionable recommendations.
